It's been a while, hasn't it? For a bit over 3 years, this website has been nothing more than a "Stay Tuned" page with no real changes to it. My hope for a website relaunch started out with wanting to release a v4 to minif's place, but with the goal to write a complete backend in PHP—along with the desire to continue self-hosting—nothing was reasonably going to happen. 

As each year passed, I slowly developed the itch to write blogposts. [Github Gists](https://gist.github.com/minif) fulfilled some of my needs to write and publish. However, my focus was on sharing tutorials, so it didn't really seem like a proper fit for personal endeavours. The need for a proper blog started to develop, and I started to change my mind on how I wanted to launch my personal site. I began considering using minif.github.io as my place to blog, and then leave the remaining functionality I wanted to a secondary website.

However, one thing that I still wanted to do was come up with the next iteration of my website's design. I wanted to continue my trend of making my next website better than my last. As such, my plan was to learn webgl and have a interactive background to showcase a fancy visual. Unfortunately, that was still a bit too ambitious, and due to the joys that is "life" (better known as working or studying full time) I never could find time to focus on properly coming up with a design. Therefore, my site sat longer and longer with nothing on it while blogpost ideas came and went.

As of today, I finally made the call to just get _something_ out, and so I decided to write a simple css style for now. The motivation likely came from watching YouTube videos about people setting up personal websites and blogs. So with my urge to finally get it made, I spent today researching how jekyll worked and constructed a simple website. Alongside the blog I wanted, I added a few typical pages for a personal website to hopefully serve as a bit of extra utility. I would still love to make a webgl based interactive background, but that will have to wait. Now, nothing will hold me back from making blogposts when I gain urges to write.

So enough about that, I should probably talk a bit about what exactly minif.github.io will be. This website will serve as a blog for me, plus a showcase of things I have worked on. I have already created pages of links to my photography and coding projects, and as I make things I will try to remember to add them here. My plans for file sharing, fun APIs, and backend-dependent games will wait for minif's place 4, whenever that is, because those features are not really technically possible with Github Pages. 

There has been a bit of a history with my usage of minif.github.io. Way back in 2015 I followed a coding tutorial on releasing a website, which I don't really have a recolection of but judging context clues it probably was a showcase of jekyll on Github Pages. Despite my desires for a personal website, for some reason I decided to abandon it as for the longest time it was nothing more than a blank jekyll template. Then sometime in early 2020 I repurposed it to be where you access minif's place, with a whole mechanism that grabs the URL of my self-hosted server to load larger assets (mainly photos and files). When I discovered that ngrok used my ip address as the URL I decided to take everything down and leave it as the blank page until I was ready to find a use.

I should also mention why I am ditching the "updates" name and going back to calling it a blog again. In 2020 I changed the focus to be about announcements on changes to my website. But is that really interesting to anybody but me? In retrospect, I regret ever making the change in the first place, as I believe I deprived myself of years of possibly interesting blogposts. Though I guess I never really used the blog much initially. Basically, I want to have a place to be a bit more personal, and also to have more internet presence in general, and hopefully this blog will solve that. 

In the coming weeks I will be writing a bunch of blog posts of things I wanted to write about ages ago. I also need to figure out what I want to do with the older blogposts (later called news posts). Also, I will be making refinements to this website a bunch, as I whipped it up today right after getting home from work. Its a bit late for me right now and I have to get up for work tomorrow so I shall post this and proofread tomorrow.

(Edit: Had a good nights sleep, long day of work, and I am back home. I have now proofread this blogpost and hopefully it reads a bit better now. Publishing without any real proofreading will probably not happen in the future but I honestly couldn't wait to have a website again.)
