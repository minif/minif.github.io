Its been a while, hasn't it? For a bit over 3 years, this website has been nothing more than a "Stay Tuned" page with no real changes to it. My hope for a relaunch started out with wanting to release a v4 to minif's place, but with the goal to write a complete backend in PHP—along with the desire to continue self-hosting—nothing was reasonably going to happen. 

As each year passed, I slowly developed the itch to write blogposts. I was sort of covered by [Github Gists](https://gist.github.com/minif) which I treated a bit like a blogging platform. However, usually those posts served a funcional and useful purpose to readers, and didn't really seem like a proper fit for personal endeavours. The need for a proper blog started to develop, and I started to change my mind on how I wanted to launch my personal site. I began considering using minif.github.io as my place to blog, and then leave the remaining functionality to a secondary website.

However, one thing that I still wanted to do was have the next iteration of a website design. Each refresh to my website I challenged myself to put more and more effort into the design. As such, my plan for the next iteration was to learn webgl and have a interactive background. Unfortunately that was still a bit too ambitious and due to the joys that is "life" I never could find time to focus on properly coming up with a design. Therefore, my site sat longer and longer with nothing on it while blogpost idea came and went.

As of today, I finally made the call to push that aspect until later too and just write a simple css style for now. My YouTube feed has included a few videos about personal websites and blogs, and that combined with reading up on jekyll at work today finally convinced me it was time. I still would love to make a webgl based interactive background, but that will have to wait. At least now it will no longer be holding me back to make blogposts as I wish.

So enough about that, I should probably talk a bit about what exactly minif.github.io will be. Way back in 2015 I followed a coding tutorial on releasing a website, and for the longest time that is what this website was. Then sometime in early 2020 I repurposed it to serve some parts of minif's place, with a whole mechanism that grabs the URL of my self-hosted server to grab larger assets. Then, when I discovered that ngrok used my ip address as the URL I decided to take it down. From now on this website will serve as a standalone blog, plus a showcase of things I have worked on. I have already created pages of links to my photography and coding projects, and as I make things I will try to remember to add more. My plans for a file dropbox, fun APIs, and backend-dependent games will wait for minif's place 4, whenever that is. 

I should also mention why I am ditching the "updates" name and going back to calling it my blog. In 2020 I changed the focus to be about announcements about changes to my websites. But is that really interesting to anybody but me? In general I regret ever making the change in the first place, as I believe I deprived myself of many years of writing. Though I guess I never really used the blog much initially. Basically, I want to have a place to be a bit more personal and have a bit more internet presence, and this blog will hopefully solve that. 

In the coming weeks I will be writing a bunch of blog posts of things I would have written ages ago. I also need to figure out what I want to do with the older blogposts (later called news posts). Also, I will be making refinements to this website a bunch, as I whipped it up today right after getting home from work. Its a bit late for me right now and I have to get up for work tomorrow so I shall post this and proofread tomorrow.
