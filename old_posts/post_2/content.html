Oct 29 2016 - Farlands

Recently, Minecraft PE 16.0 was released recently. It included commands, allowing you to teleport. But with the infinite generation, the far lands exist. Now, you can get there using the teleport command.

To get there, go /teleport 12550825 100 0 (You can change the last number to whatever you want or also to 12550825)

You will either end up or be near a strange land with distorted tunnels

Many objects, like torches that don’t take up a full box will appear flat.

Moving around requires sprinting, and you can go through all the blocks. The caves go all the way up to the sky limit.

Around sea level, a huge ocean will form, stretching to the bedrock with no air.
