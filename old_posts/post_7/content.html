February 10 2021 - News Page Incompatibility Warning

Minif's Place 3.2 will feature changes to the news page. Unfortunately, these changes warranted a migration into a new, more efficient format. This means that Minif's Place 3.0 and 3.1 will no longer be able to view the current news.

Under 99% of circumstances, this should not affect you. However, in the case you are still using Minif's Place 3.0 or Minif's Place 3.1, this post serves as a warning to upgrade to Minif's Place 3.2 or above.

(November 23 2021) UPDATE: I have once again begun overhauling the news posts section. Minif's Place 3.2.1 and 3.2 will also no longer function. It is recommended to use Minif's Place 3.3 or above.
